# ⚙️ ML Workflow

## 📌 Overview

Machine Learning Workflow is the **step-by-step process** of building an ML model.

---

## 🧠 Core Idea

> Follow a structured pipeline from data to prediction

---

## 🔷 Complete Workflow

```text
Data Collection
   ↓
Data Cleaning
   ↓
EDA (Exploratory Data Analysis)
   ↓
Feature Engineering
   ↓
Train-Test Split
   ↓
Model Training
   ↓
Evaluation
   ↓
Hyperparameter Tuning
   ↓
Deployment
```

---

## 🔷 Explanation

### 1. Data Collection

* Gather raw data

---

### 2. Data Cleaning

* Handle missing values
* Remove duplicates

---

### 3. EDA

* Understand patterns
* Visualizations

---

### 4. Feature Engineering

* Encoding
* Scaling

---

### 5. Model Training

* Apply ML algorithms

---

### 6. Evaluation

* Metrics (Accuracy, F1, etc.)

---

### 7. Deployment

* Use model in real world

---

## 🔷 Why Workflow is Important

* Avoids mistakes
* Ensures consistency
* Makes project structured

---

## ⚠️ Important Points

* Always split before training
* Avoid data leakage
* Validate model properly

---

## 🧠 Interview Insight

👉 Question:

## 🧠 One-Line Summary

> ML workflow is the structured process of building and deploying machine learning models.
